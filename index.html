Parramatta Explorer

An accessible plan for every City of Parramatta event, so you know you can get in before you leave home.

For people with disability, older residents and families, deciding whether to go to an event usually means guessing whether they can actually get in, move around and take part.

That affects wheelchair users and people with mobility needs, people who are blind or have low vision, Deaf and hard-of-hearing people, autistic and sensory-sensitive people, older residents, carers and families, and culturally diverse communities.

Community benefit

When people can trust the information, more of them take part.

Deciding whether to go to an event often means guessing. If you can check the access details first, you don't waste a trip, and you're more likely to go.

The same information helps Council. When it can see which access details are missing, it can fix those gaps for the next event.

How we'll measure it

Not just how many people came, but how many events have verified access details, how many gaps we close, and what people say afterwards about whether they could take part.

Team

Who we are

Idhika Vaidya is a data scientist and a Parramatta local. Eraj Basheer is an IT and Cybersecurity student.

Together we want a Parramatta where everyone, including disabled people, older residents and families, can show up to an event knowing they'll be able to take part.

Our vision is an events scene where accessibility information is open and honest, and where the community and Council improve it together. We'd like to contribute a simple tool that gives people confidence before they travel, and gives Council a clear, live picture of where the barriers really are.

Privacy & responsible AI

AI helps — but a person always decides

Privacy

No accounts for attendees, no health information collected. The needs you choose stay on your device and are never sent to a server.

Tools we used

OpenAI to build the website, and ChatGPT for the HTML design and colour scheme.

Human check

AI only suggests an access attribute or a risk. Nothing is published until a person confirms it, and each item shows its source and when it was last checked.

Risks we manage

Stale or incomplete data, over-trust in AI suggestions, and exclusion when data is missing — handled by showing provenance and openly showing what we don't know.

Council's role

We'd ask Council to partner and advocate

Partner: share event and venue information, and give us access to the spaces so the plan is accurate.

Advocate: encourage venues, organisers and partners to publish accessible information and adopt the practice.

We're not asking Council to deliver or fund a new platform, or to regulate — a small, shared pilot is enough to prove the value.
